# Interface direction

A compact audio utility, not a marketing landing page. The working area should dominate; model selection is metadata, not branding.

- Neutral light workstation surface, cobalt for primary actions. No acid-green on near-black, decorative gradients, headset illustration, oversized slogan, or repeated rounded cards.
- One workbench: firmware source on the left, actual resource selection and audio editor on the right. Preserve hierarchy through spacing and restrained dividers.
- Actual file names remain visible. Human labels are annotations and do not decide replacement paths.
- Russian, English and Simplified Chinese share the same controls and state. Switching language must not discard selected audio or firmware.
- No fabricated metrics or claims that every model is hardware-tested. Hardware proof and static inspection stay distinct.
- At narrow widths, firmware selection precedes the editor without hiding important checks.

References reviewed at the owner's request:

- [Anthropic frontend-design guidance](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md)
- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable)
